# My Favorite Series: Brooklyn Nine-Nine

Honestly, **Brooklyn Nine-Nine** is my ultimate comfort show. *Whenever I'm stressed out from school or just need a good laugh, this is the first thing I put on.* It's a comedy about a group of detectives in New York's 99th Precinct, and the cast chemistry is unmatched.

---

## Quick Info

| Thing | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Genre** | Sitcom / Cop Comedy |
| **Where to Watch** | Netflix |
| **Best Running Gag** | The Halloween Heists (`Title of your sex tape` is a close second) |

## Why I Keep Rewatching It
- **The characters actually grow:** Jake starts off super immature, and watching him grow up over the seasons is actually really sweet.
- **Zero forced drama:** Unlike a lot of other sitcoms, the characters genuinely support each other.
- **Cold opens:** The 1-minute jokes before the theme song starts are legendary (especially the Backstreet Boys lineup scene).

## My Top 3 Characters
1. Captain Raymond Holt (his deadpan humor gets me every time)
2. Jake Peralta
3. Rosa Diaz
